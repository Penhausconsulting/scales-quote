How to take the photo so the price is right

  1. Stand back far enough to show the whole wall or space, not just the spot.
  2. Hold something of a known size against the wall. A dollar bill is 6.14 inches long, and a standard credit card is 3.37 inches. The photo uses it to judge scale.
  3. Take a second photo close up, with the tape measure visible if you have one.
  4. Turn the light on. Dark photos hide the water damage and the soft spots.
